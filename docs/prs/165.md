## RED

### GREEN plan

- **`stops`:** settled means a stable window of `stable` entries with
  the same best move, or the last |score| (unsigned) at least `decided`.
  The cutoff is `Duration::from_millis(start_ms).checked_mul(min(share,
  100))`, divided by 100; `None` never stops.
- **The iterative loop** keeps the history only when `early` is not
  `OFF`. With exits off it allocates nothing and reads the clock no more
  than now (implementation-review note).
- **Policies:** `CLOCK_POLICIES` as in the design table; `variant`;
  `allot_with` generalizing `allot`, which calls it with variant 0.
- **`SearchAgent`** stores its policy. After the first `clock`, a forced
  policy with one legal move returns it without searching.

## RED review response

- **Cap 3 against cap 2 (major):** new `variant_cap`: at e 44, T 18 and
  R 34 s, variant 3 must give a target of 4,000 ms and a deadline of
  8,500 ms. Cap 2 would give 8,000.
- **Both settled rules (major):** new `stops_both`, with
  `Early { 3, 2000, 25 }`.
  - At the 250 ms cutoff: an unstable history with a decided score stops,
    a stable history with an open score stops, and neither does not.
  - At 249,999,999 ns, neither of the first two stops.
  - `stops_decided` now also covers `i32::MIN` and `i32::MAX`, so the
    magnitude is unsigned across the whole input range.
- **The agent's own allocation (major):** new `agent_variant_limits`.
  - A variant-3 `clock_agent` given fixed limits of 1,234 ms reports
    exactly those, with exits off, until its first `clock`.
  - After `clock(34 s)` at 44 empties it reports 4,000/8,500 with exits
    (3, 2000, 25). That differs from variant 0's allocation, so an agent
    stuck on variant 0 fails.
- **Results:** `tests/early.rs` 15 tests: 13 fail against the stubs, and
  2 pass (`early_off_default`, `exact_ignores_early`). Clippy and
  rustfmt are clean.

## GREEN

### Changes (`src/search.rs`, `src/agents.rs`)

- **`stops`:**
  - stable when `stable > 0` and the last `stable` history entries share
    one best move;
  - decided when `decided > 0` and the last score's `unsigned_abs()` is
    at least `decided`;
  - the cutoff is `Duration::from_millis(start_ms).checked_mul(min(share,
    100))` divided by 100. An overflow (`NO_DEADLINE` with a share above
    0) gives `None`, which is never reached.
- **Iterative deepening:** after each completed iteration, only when
  `limits.early != Early::OFF`, it pushes `(best, score)` and calls
  `stops` with `start.elapsed()`. With exits off, the empty `Vec` never
  allocates and the clock is read no more often than before. The exact
  branch is untouched.
- **Policies:**
  - `CLOCK_POLICIES` is built with a private `const fn ClockPolicy::new`,
    with reserve 4 throughout, as in the design table;
  - `Early::exits(decided)` gives (3, decided, 25);
  - `ClockPolicy::variant(k)` is `CLOCK_POLICIES.get(k)`.
- **`allot_with`** generalizes the allocator: weight `num/den` for
  e ≤ 44, `moves = ceil((e − T)/2) + reserve`, and `cap · t` capped at
  R/4; it sets `early`. `allot` is `allot_with` with variant 0. The
  bound is products below 2 × 3 × 2^40.
- **`SearchAgent`** holds an `Option<&'static ClockPolicy>` (`None` for
  `search_agent`) and the remaining clock. `limits` uses `allot_with`
  under the policy once a clock has been given. `choose` plays a lone
  legal move without searching when the agent is clocked, has had a
  clock, and its policy is forced.
