# Issue 160: Time management design

Design work for epic #151, with no test stage: `docs/design/time.md`, for
review before any code. No child issues have been created; they follow
approval, as the epic requires.

## Summary

- **Engine:**
  - `SearchLimits.start_ms`: no iteration starts after it. `new` and
    `depth` set it equal to `time_ms`, so flat play is unchanged.
  - `allot(board, remaining, threshold)`: stage-weighted allocation, with
    a solve reserve and a worst case of R/2 per move, apart from the
    existing unbounded fallback.
  - `Agent::clock` (a default no-op) and `clock_agent`.
  - About 25–35 lines.
- **Tools:**
  - a clock belongs to an agent (`pc:80:c<ms>`). The driver times both
    sides in-process, a clocked side that goes below zero flags and
    loses, and load voiding covers the clock control;
  - a `+book` suffix for #156;
  - journal fields `black_ms white_ms flag`.
  - **Tools budget:** raised (user decision). I propose a ceiling of 1,400.
- **Gate:**
  - the candidate's clock C equals today's flat player's measured mean
    thinking time per game, taken from a pre-registered 50-pair pilot
    (#142 evidence: 52.8 s);
  - the baseline is `pc:80:3000`, unclocked;
  - 400 pairs on a fresh `bench/openings_time.txt`, one look, lower bound
    above 0.500;
  - about 6 h.

## Evidence

Per-side thinking time from `docs/tuning/threshold_142_match.journal`
(400 side-games at 3,000 ms per move): mean 52.8 s, median 52.0 s, 90th
percentile 55.3 s, max 63.4 s. The longest move was 12.0 s, the 4×
exact-solve allowance.

## User input folded in

- **Tools budget:** "Raise tools budget".
- **Fairness** (user, Nov 18): "the 3-second-per-move baseline only uses
  about 53 of its 90 seconds, so the new player gets more thinking time."
  The draft's equal 90 s clocks were replaced. The candidate's whole clock
  is now the baseline's measured mean usage, and the baseline is
  unclocked.
