The lower bound of the 95% pair-bootstrap interval of the candidate's mean
score is **above 0.5** (equivalently the Elo lower bound is above 0).
10,000 resamples; seed 126. This is one look.

The match is about three and a half hours. It runs detached from a
dedicated worktree pinned at the approved commit, on a quiet machine. The
log and journal are copied into the development checkout and committed:
`docs/tuning/pattern_gate_3000.log` and
`docs/tuning/pattern_gate_3000.journal`. The reviewer recomputes the
result from the journal with `report`.

- `SearchConfig::standard()` uses `pattern::evaluate`.

  `classic`.
- The `pattern` agent name is removed.
- `tools/examples/tune.rs` is deleted.

- The switch stays off and nothing is deleted.
- The result is recorded. No single cause is assumed: the learning report,
