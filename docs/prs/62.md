## Intended behaviour

`src/score.rs` exposes the centidisc score contract:

- `SCORE_SCALE = 100`, `INF = 1_000_000`, `EVAL_BOUND = 6_400`.
- `terminal_score(wthor_diff: i32) = wthor_diff * SCORE_SCALE`.
- The documented objective maximises the WTHOR final score: sign gives outcome
  priority, magnitude gives margin, and heuristic values estimate the same
  quantity.
- A heuristic `+6400` may outrank a small proven win; exact terminal values are
  never approximated.

No `Board` wiring is tested here; that arrives in Phase 1.
