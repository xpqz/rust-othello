# Issue 175: GUI engine levels: random, greedy, one-ply evaluation and full search

## RED

Request: human-friendly levels in the GUI. Level 0 plays a random legal
move, level 1 the greedy move, level 2 the best move available without
search, and level 3 the current timed search.

Interpretation of level 2: the legal move whose position after one ply
has the best static evaluation for the mover, `-evaluate(play(board, s))`,
with no search below it.

Intended behaviour:

- New module `gui/src/levels.rs` (GUI crate; the engine and its
  1,500-line budget are untouched). `Level` has variants `Random`,
  `Greedy`, `Static` and `Search`. `Level::ALL` lists them weakest first,
  and `label` gives the panel text "0 · Random move", "1 · Greedy",
  "2 · Best evaluation" and "3 · Full search".
- `quick_move(level, board, rng)` returns the level's move for levels
  0 to 2: uniform over the legal moves with `othello::rng::Rng`, the
  engine's `GreedyAgent` choice, and the one-ply evaluation argmax. It
  returns `None` at `Search` or without a legal move. In RED it is a stub
  returning `None`.
- `App` gains `level` (default `Search`, so the GUI's current play is
  unchanged) and `rng`, seeded from the system clock. In GREEN, levels 0
  to 2 play their move at once on the engine's turn, without a worker
  thread, a clock charge or a "Last engine search" report. Level 3 starts
  the timed search as before.
- In GREEN the side panel gets a level selector under "Engine strength".
  The clock and solve-threshold sliders are disabled below level 3,
  where they have no effect. A level change takes effect at the engine's
  next move, not only in a new game.

The RED fields carry a temporary `#[allow(dead_code)]`, which GREEN
removes.

Tests written:

- `gui/tests/levels.rs`
  - `random_legal_varied`: 200 seeded level-0 picks at the start position
    are legal, cover all four opening moves, and repeat for a repeated
    seed.
  - `greedy_matches_agent`: on every position of a seeded random game,
    level 1 equals `greedy_agent().choose`.
  - `static_best_evaluation`: on every position of a seeded random game,
    level 2 is legal and has the best one-ply value, and it differs from
    greedy somewhere.
  - `search_and_stuck_none`: level 3 returns `None`, and so does every
    level with no legal move. This is a contract guard that already
    passes against the stub.
- `gui/src/main.rs`
  - `level_default_search`: a new app starts at level 3. It already
    passes.
  - `quick_levels_move`: at each of levels 0 to 2, with the engine
    opening, one `step` makes a legal move (levels 1 and 2: their level's
    move) with no search started, the clock uncharged, no search report,
    and the human to move.
  - `search_level_thinks`: at level 3 the engine's turn starts a search
    and makes no move yet. It already passes, guarding the current path.

Expected failures (`cargo test --release --locked --no-fail-fast
--manifest-path gui/Cargo.toml`):

- `random_legal_varied`, `greedy_matches_agent`, `static_best_evaluation`
  panic on the stub's `None`.
- `quick_levels_move` panics at its `thinking.is_none()` assertion,
  because every level still searches.

All other GUI tests pass: 9 of 10 in the binary, 1 of 4 in `levels`, and
6 of 6 in `overlays`. GUI clippy with warnings denied passes.
