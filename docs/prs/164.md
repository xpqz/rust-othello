# Issue 164: Time management design amendment 1, early exits and a tuning stage

Design work with no test stage: an amendment to `docs/design/time.md`,
for review before any code or tuning game.

## Why

- The user chose a pre-registered tuning stage before the clock gate
  (#163), because the allocation constants are untested guesses and the
  gate gets one look per opening set.
- The user added: "We should not stick to a 'fixed time budget' -- if you
  find a move quicker, take it, and yield. That makes for more natural
  play."

## Summary

- **Early exits, for clocked play only:**
  - a forced move is played without search;
  - a settled search stops starting iterations once the best move has
    been stable for k iterations, or the score is decided, and at least
    a share of the start cutoff has passed;
  - saved time stays on the clock for later moves.
- **Four registered variants** (`CLOCK_POLICIES`):
  - 0: the approved allocator;
  - 1: plus the forced move and the stable exit;
  - 2: plus the decided-score exit;
  - 3: plus bolder midgame allocation.

  Selected as `c<ms>v<k>`.
- **Tuning stage:**
  - a 750 ms pilot sets the tuning clock `C_t`;
  - each variant plays 200 pairs against `pc:80:750` on the tune
    openings;
  - pick: the highest score among the variants that meet equal time,
    ties to the lower number, and variant 0 if none qualifies;
  - only the pick is gated, on the untouched `openings_time.txt`.
- **Flat play is unchanged.**
- **Estimated size:** about 30 engine lines (1,341 of 1,500 now) and 8
  tools lines (1,478 of 1,500).
