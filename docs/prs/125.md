  `eval_classic`, with `classic_config()` at threshold 14.

### Measurements for GREEN

All matches use `bench/openings_tune.txt`, never the gate openings; logs
and journals go under `docs/tuning/` with commit, date and elapsed time.

- Nanoseconds per `evaluate` call, each evaluator, over the bench corpus
  by stage.
- Nodes per second and depth reached at 3 s over the corpus.
- `pattern` against `classic` at equal depth and at equal nodes, 100 pairs
  each.
- Carried from #124: over the corpus and over positions sampled from
  search at 3 s, report how often the raw sum exceeds `EVAL_BOUND` and the
  share of looked-up entries with fewer than 20 training occurrences. If
  either is common, stronger damping (a larger floor with the horizon
  kept) is the first remedy; that is a re-fit, not new code.
- If the pattern search rate is below half the classic rate, improve the
  indexing within the tools/engine budget and measure again before the
  gate issue.

The notes will give a short reading of prediction quality against cost,
whatever the result. Tools production is 1,173 before this issue and has a
1,300-line budget; the classic copy and agents must fit.

The equal-depth, equal-node and per-call measurements do not depend on
load. The 3 s rate measurement is the one that waits for a quiet machine;
it runs detached in a worktree pinned at the approved commit. If the
pattern search rate is below half the classic rate, I stop, report the
measurement and propose the indexing change before implementing it; that
change gets its own RED.

The trainer example reports the frequency of raw sums beyond
`EVAL_BOUND` and the share of looked-up entries with fewer than 20
training occurrences, over the corpus and over the search-sampled

The equal-nodes match is at **1,000,000** nodes. `search_rate` reports,
per evaluator, nodes per second and depth for each corpus position and
the medians, single-threaded, one position at a time, and states how many
positions were measured. It waits for a quiet machine and a pinned
worktree; it is not started here.

## GREEN review: search rate and rulings

The reviewer ran the pre-registered `search_rate` command unchanged at
`838c8e2` on a quiet machine. The log is committed as
`docs/tuning/pattern_search_rate.log`.

```text
classic positions 20 median_nps 3141479 median_depth 11
pattern positions 20 median_nps 2709403 median_depth 10
```

Over the 12 positions per evaluator that used the full three seconds
(the rest solved early): classic **2.13 M nodes/s**, mean depth 11.3;
pattern **1.86 M nodes/s**, mean depth 10.7. The pattern search runs at
about **87%** of the classic rate. The per-call cost is four times higher,
but the evaluator is a small part of a node. This is well above the
half-rate guideline, so **no indexing work** is added.

Rulings:

- **No re-fit before the gate.** 11% of sampled lookups are entries seen
  fewer than 20 times, and raw sums beyond the bound are 1 in 199. With
  the pattern agent scoring 83% at equal depth and 84% at equal nodes,
  there is no measured problem to justify changing the weights. The gate
  runs on the committed asset `676987…ea8cb`.
- **Reading:** the tables cost about two thirds of a ply at 3 s and are
  worth roughly 220 to 350 Elo at equal depth on the tune set. The gate
  measures the net at equal time.

`./scripts/check.sh` passes: engine 1,099, tools 1,284, trainer 1,150.
