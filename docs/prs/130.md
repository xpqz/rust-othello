## Bridge check

`tools/examples/edax_ladder.rs --bridge` played the first ten openings
from `bench/openings_tune.txt`, both colours, 20 games. It used the greedy
agent against Edax level 1, so the result is deliberately not a strength
measurement. The bridge check verifies:

- coordinates round-trip in both directions;
- our replay and Edax agree on legality;
- passes occur (119 pass plies across the 20 games) and the explicit pass
  probes check Edax's `genmove` pass output and our `play ... ps` input;
- every final disc count agrees with our own replay (20/20).

Log: `docs/tuning/edax_bridge.log`. The log records the commit, date,
Edax path and both SHA-256 values.

## Driver

`tools/examples/edax_ladder.rs` is outside the tools production count.
It has three modes:

- `--bridge`: the 20-game check above.
- `--level N`: the 50-pair level run, journaled.
- `--ladder`: levels 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 in ascending order, stopping
  after the first level whose mean score is below 0.25.

The journal is one file per level, default
`docs/tuning/edax_level<N>.journal`. Records are appended per pair; a
pair is replayed if either game is missing, or if the measured load
average exceeds `--max-load` (void records are appended, then discarded
and replayed). Re-running the same command resumes from the journal.
`--jobs` defaults to 4 and `--max-load` to 6.

The pilot used the release example at 3,000 ms per engine move.

## Pre-registration (before the first ladder game)

- Engine: `SearchConfig::standard()`, 3,000 ms per move, at engine commit
  `13ff240` (this branch changes no engine source; the journal also records
  the driver commit at launch).
- Edax: the binary and eval data above, single-threaded, no opening book,
  fixed `-level`.
- Levels: 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, ascending. Stop after the first level with
  mean score below 0.25 and record the rest as not played.
- Openings: the first 50 non-comment lines of `bench/openings_tune.txt`,
  both colours, 50 pairs per level. Not the gate openings.
- Concurrency: 4 jobs, `--max-load 6`; pairs above the limit are void and
  replayed. Run on a quiet machine under `caffeinate`, detached from a
  pinned worktree.
- Estimate: the 2-pair level-1 pilot took 118.6 s with 2 jobs. At 4 jobs
  that is about 25 minutes per level and about 3 hours for all seven
  levels; higher Edax levels add search time, and the stop rule may end
  the ladder earlier.
- Pilot result: 4/4 wins at level 1, mean 1.0000, interval
  [0.0397, 1.0000], mean disc difference +42.50. Log and journal:
  `docs/tuning/edax_pilot.log`, `docs/tuning/edax_pilot.journal`.

## Planned report

Per level: wins, draws, losses, mean score, 95% pair-bootstrap interval
(10,000 resamples, seed 1), mean disc difference and Edax mean time per
move. The 50% level by linear interpolation between the bracketing level
means, with both intervals, stated as "equivalent to Edax level L,
single-threaded, no book", not an Elo rating.

Loss attribution is a post-run pass. The journal already stores every
move, the engine colour and the result, so I will replay each lost game to
the first position with 24 or fewer empties, ask Edax for its exact value
at that position, and count losses already decided there (midgame) versus
losses after it (endgame, down to our exact solve at 14). The attribution
and the reading against selective search, deeper endgame solving and
training data will be added before the final report.
