## Phase design (pinned before implementation)

- `phase(empties) = (60 - clamp(empties, 12, 60)) * 1000 / 48`, so
  opening = 0, endgame = 1000, linear between.
- Feature weights per thousand, interpolated by phase:
  - mobility: 500 -> 800
  - frontier raw multiplier: 10 -> 4
  - corner risk: 1000 -> 700
  - positional table (including corners): 1000 throughout
  - disc difference: `phase / 10` -> 0 at opening, 100 at endgame, so disc
    diff is active in the heuristic region above the solver threshold.
- `evaluate` combines the table, scaled mobility, frontier multiplier,
  signed corner risk, and phase-weighted disc difference into centidiscs,
  then debug-asserts and clamps to `EVAL_BOUND`.

These are hand-chosen starting weights; #99 owns tuning/ablation.

Existing `single_disc_values`, `mixed_table_sum`, and frozen corpus
snapshots will change under the phase combination; GREEN will update them and
regenerate the corpus with provenance.
