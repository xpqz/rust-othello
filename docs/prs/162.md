# Issue 162: Tools clock control

Tools side of `docs/design/time.md` (approved, revision 3): § Tools and
§ Tests › Tools.

## RED

### Scaffolding

- `harness::play_clocked(opening, black, white, clocks, elapsed)`, with
  `Clocked { result, used, flag }` and `clocked_points`. These are stubs:
  `play_clocked` plays an unclocked `play_game`, charges nothing and never
  flags, and `clocked_points` returns the disc result. `elapsed` is the
  injected time source: it receives the `Instant` at which a `choose`
  started and returns the duration to charge.
- `agents::clock(name) -> Result<Option<u64>, String>`, a stub that
  returns `Ok(None)`.
- **Tools budget raised to 1,400** (user decision, recorded in the
  design), consistently in `tools/tests/budget.rs`, `AGENTS.md`
  (Engineering constraints) and `docs/PLAN.md`: the budget table, a dated
  note under it, and the present-tense mentions at lines 26, 42, 408 and
  498. The engine's `src/lib.rs` header comment is updated to the current
  ceilings: tools 1,400 and trainer 2,500. The stubs take the tools crate
  to 1,318 lines.

### Choices beyond the design text, for review

- **`greedy:c<ms>` and `random:c<ms>` are accepted.** These agents ignore
  `clock`, but the driver still charges and flags them. That gives a fully
  deterministic clocked fixture for the CLI tests (flags at a known move,
  and SIGKILL and resume with identical records). A clocked engine agent
  searches against real time and cannot provide one. `+book` stays refused
  on both kinds, and on `greedy:c<ms>`.
- **`OTHELLO_TOOLS_ELAPSED_MS`:** when set, the driver charges that fixed
  number of ms for every `choose`, in place of the measured time. It
  mirrors the existing `OTHELLO_TOOLS_LOAD` and is how the CLI tests
  script time.
- **`--nodes` with a clocked greedy agent is allowed**, since greedy has no
  search to limit. `c<ms>` on a search agent with `--nodes` is refused, as
  the design requires. `control()` is `clock` whenever either agent has a
  clock.
- **There is no move list in this driver's journal.** The design's
  "`moves` without the discarded move" applies to `version_match`, not
  here. A flag is visible through `plies` in the harness result and through
  `flag` and the charged times in the journal.
- **Report:** `othello-tools report` adds a line
  `time a <ms> b <ms> flags a <n> b <n>`, giving each agent's mean charged
  ms per game (rounded down) and its flag count.
- **Void events:** stderr prints `void pair <p> load <l>`, with the
  three-decimal load.
- **Deferred to #163:** the gate's paired time difference and its
  interval. The journal holds the per-game times it needs.

### GREEN plan

- **Harness:** `play_clocked` charges `elapsed(start)` per `choose`,
  accumulates `Duration`s, and calls `clock(remaining ms, rounded down)`
  before each move of a clocked side. A side flags on `used > C`: the
  game stops with that move unplayed. `play_game` becomes
  `play_clocked` with no clocks.
- **Grammar:** a `c<ms>` time field (1..=`MAX_CLOCK_MS`), an optional
  `+book` suffix, and `clock(name)`. `build` gives clocked search kinds a
  `clock_agent`.
- **Driver:**
  - control `clock`, voided like `time`;
  - load and `--max-load` rounded to three decimals before every
    comparison and in the header;
  - each game appends `black_ms white_ms flag` after
    `points … load … jobs …`;
  - the stderr void line;
  - the report's time line;
  - `OTHELLO_TOOLS_ELAPSED_MS`.

## RED review response

`tools/tests/clock.rs` is rewritten: 20 tests plus one ignored smoke
test. All 20 fail against the stubs.

- **Load rounding (major):**
  - `load_rounded` now uses a sample of 6.0004 against a limit of 5.9996.
    The two differ raw but both round to 6.000, so an unrounded limit
    fails the test.
  - Checked: the pair is accepted live, the header has `max_load 6.000`,
    and the journal has `load 6.000`. A resume leaves the journal byte for
    byte unchanged, and the standalone report includes both pairs. 6.0006
    is void.
  - It runs for clocked play (`ab:2:c10`) and flat timed play (`ab:2:1`).
- **Partial-pair recovery (major):**
  - New `half_pair_recovered`: a 3-pair journal is cut to records 0:0,
    0:1 and 1:0, then resumed at a different load (1.000 before, 2.000
    after). Checked by record id: pair 0 keeps load 1.000; 1:0 is replayed
    with its mate 1:1; pair 2 is new.
  - `clock_kill_resume` stays, now with `ab:7` (depth-limited) against a
    clocked `ab:2:c10` charged 20 ms per move, so the clocked side flags on
    its first move. Records are compared by id.
  - On a timeout waiting for progress, `fail` kills and reaps the child
    before panicking.
- **Engine wiring (major):**
  - New public `agents::search(name, nodes, ordering) -> SearchAgent`,
    which `build` will use for search kinds.
  - `clocked_wiring` checks that `search("pc:80:c50000")` follows
    `clock(40 s)` and `clock(20 s)` with exactly `allot`'s limits, while
    `pc:80:3000` keeps 3,000 ms after `clock`. No real time is involved.
  - The real-time `engine_clock_smoke` is now `#[ignore]`d, as an optional
    smoke test.
- **Stopped-board score (major):**
  - **Definition:** the stopped board's disc lead in centidiscs, from
    Black's side, with no empties awarded.
  - `black_flags` and `white_flags` assert it.
  - New `stopped_score` finds a flag point where Black is ahead and the
    discarded move would change the lead. It checks that Black still loses
    (0 points) while the score stays positive.
  - `flags_scored` checks each journal `score` against the stopped board,
    computed from the opening.
  - New `book_charged`: a book agent's first move is the book move, and
    every call, that one included, is charged 7 ms.
  - New `passes_free`: a seeded random game that does pass, with no charge
    for passes.
- **Grammar:** kept to the approved design.
  - `greedy:c…` and `random:c…` are now refused (in `clock_grammar`).
  - No clock-plus-`--nodes` exception: `ab:2:c100` with `--nodes` is
    refused.
  - The deterministic fixtures instead use a clocked search agent that
    flags on its first move under the injected charge.
- **Injected time and identity:** when `OTHELLO_TOOLS_ELAPSED_MS` is set,
  the journal header gains `elapsed <ms>`. New `clock_identity` refuses a
  resume with a changed clock, a changed charge, or no charge.
- **Report:** `time a <ms> b <ms> flags a <n> b <n>` (`flags_scored`
  expects `time a 20 b 10 flags a 4 b 0`).

**Results:** the tools suite gives 72 passed and 20 failed, all in
`tests/clock.rs` and all from the stubs, plus 1 ignored. Clippy and
rustfmt are clean. Tools are at 1,322 of 1,400 lines, with the new
`agents::search` stub.

## GREEN

### Changes

- **`tools/src/harness.rs`:**
  - **`clocked_from` is the single game loop.** Before each move of a
    clocked side it calls `clock(remaining)`, with the remaining time
    rounded down to whole ms and saturating at 0. It times each `choose`
    with the injected `elapsed(start)` and accumulates `Duration`s per
    side. When charged time exceeds the clock, strictly, the game stops
    with that move unplayed. `score` is then the stopped board's lead
    (`terminal_score` of the disc difference, from Black's side).
  - `play_clocked` applies the opening and calls `clocked_from`.
    `play_game` and `play_from` are `play_clocked`/`clocked_from` with no
    clocks and real time, so existing callers are unchanged.
  - `clocked_points` gives a flagged game to the opponent.
- **`tools/src/agents.rs`:**
  - `+book` suffix (refused on `random` and `greedy`); a `c<ms>` time
    field in 1..=`MAX_CLOCK_MS` via `time_field`;
  - `clock(name)`;
  - `config` sets `book`, and the ProbCut margin ignores the suffix;
  - `search(name)` gives a `clock_agent` for clocked names, and `build`
    uses it.
- **`tools/src/main.rs`:**
  - `control()` is `clock` when either agent has a clock. `void()` covers
    `time` and `clock`, and so does the default `--max-load`.
  - `rounded()`: the sampled load and `--max-load` are rounded to three
    decimals before any comparison and before they are written.
  - `Run::parse` validates both names up front (a clock with `--nodes`
    is refused even with `--pairs 0`) and reads
    `OTHELLO_TOOLS_ELAPSED_MS`. When set, the header ends with
    `elapsed <ms>`.
  - Games run through `play_clocked`. Under `time` and `clock` control,
    each journal line ends with `black_ms <ms> white_ms <ms> flag …`, and
    `report` prints `time a <ms> b <ms> flags a <n> b <n>`. Under `depth`
    and `nodes` control they are left out, so those journals and reports
    stay reproducible: the existing determinism and resume tests in
    `tools/tests/journal.rs` require identical output, and failed when
    measured times were written for every control.
  - Every void pair over the limit prints `void pair <p> load <l>` to
    stderr.

### Budget (user decision)

The clock control came to 1,446 tools lines against the 1,400 ceiling;
the design estimated 70–100 added lines, and it took about 154. Asked
whether to raise, trim or drop the report line, the user chose
"Raise to 1,500". `tools/tests/budget.rs`, `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PLAN.md`
(the table, the dated note and the present-tense mentions) and the
`src/lib.rs` header are consistent at 1,500.
