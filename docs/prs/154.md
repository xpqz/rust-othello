## Commands

Run from the worktree root, wrapped in `caffeinate -i`:

1. Pilot (defaults: sample 200, seed 150, depths 18,19,20, threads 5,
   limit 36 h):
   `othello-trainer book pilot data/wthor run/pilot.journal --jobs 5`.
   `--jobs 5` matches the 5 threads the projection divides by, so the times
   are measured under the same parallel load as the scoring run.
2. Scoring at the D the rule selects:
   `othello-trainer book score data/wthor run/score.journal --depth D --jobs 5`.
3. Build:
   `othello-trainer book build data/wthor run/score.journal --roots bench/openings_book.txt --asset assets/book.bin --manifest docs/tuning/book_manifest.txt`.

Both long jobs resume by re-running the same command. The outputs (logs,
journals, asset and manifest) are copied into this branch and committed.

## Review response (setup and pilot reviews)

Correction, replacing the setup note's "can only overstate" and the
pilot note's "times are therefore overstated": the pilot timings were
observed under shared load (implementor's observation, peak load average
about 11) and may differ on a quiet machine. The effect of load was not
isolated or measured. The projections are search-time sizing estimates
under that load, not a guaranteed upper bound on end-to-end builder
runtime; the pilot excludes table allocation, clearing and other builder
overhead. The roughly 26.5% reduction that D = 20 would have needed is
arithmetic only. D = 19 stands as registered and unadjusted.

### Why coverage is thin (analysis, no change made)

Joining the scoring journal's best drop-out square to each stored record:

- 1,843 of the 3,771 stored records (49%) choose a drop-out move, one that
  leaves the scored node set. Every line through such a record ends there.
- 187 of the 214 roots with `first 1` choose a drop-out at the root itself.
  The other 27 choose a book move but no engine-to-move position after the
  opponent's book replies is stored.
- The 15,106 nodes not stored are reachable only through a move the
  scored book would never play. Reachability pruning removes them;
  `room` removed nothing.

The design permits this: the engine plays the higher value, book or
drop-out. Its scale was not predicted. One possible contributor, not
tested: each node's drop-out value is the maximum over several independent
searches, so noise favours a drop-out when values are close.
