## Intended implementation

`tools/examples/fit_probcut.rs` will implement the staged, journaled pipeline:
self-play games, deterministic line/variation sampling, per-position
measurements through the public `negamax`/`Ctx`/table API, OLS and σ-model fits,
held-out decision calibration with Clopper-Pearson bounds, the supported-domain
selection, the d′ comparison, and the final `ProbCut::new(...)` line in
`docs/tuning/probcut_fit.log`.

Interrupt/resume coverage, including SIGKILL mid-measurement, truncated-tail
replay and header mismatch, will be added with the pipeline implementation.
