## Intended implementation

`tools/examples/fit_probcut.rs` will implement the staged, journaled pipeline:
self-play games, deterministic line/variation sampling, per-position
measurements through the public `negamax`/`Ctx`/table API, OLS and σ-model fits,
held-out decision calibration with Clopper-Pearson bounds, the supported-domain
selection, the d′ comparison, and the final `ProbCut::new(...)` line in
`docs/tuning/probcut_fit.log`.

Interrupt/resume coverage, including SIGKILL mid-measurement, truncated-tail
replay and header mismatch, will be added with the pipeline implementation.

## Interim GREEN: pipeline checkpoint, revision 2

Addressed the calibration review:

- σ candidates are weighted fits (Linear, EdaxStyle, StageTable), with per-cell
  95%-floor checks and selection by held-out decision error then constants.
- Decision rates are per `(depth, stage)` cell with CP bounds; the supported
  domain is the largest rectangle of cells with ≥200 fired decisions per
  direction and ≤12% upper bounds at t=1.5.
- Variation windows come from iterative depth-1..D searches using the engine's
  `ordered_moves`; the log reports the infinite-bound fraction.
- The sampler never pads; sampled/requested counts are logged.
- d′ error and node-cost comparison is logged for shallow, d−2 and d−4.
- The `PROBCUT_FIT` line is printed only for a supported rectangle; otherwise
  the reason is logged.
- Cells with n < 30 are marked insufficient.
- Two measurement tiers are supported (`--positions` to d≤10,
  `--deep-positions` to d≤12); progress is time-based.
- Full-run rule predeclared: use global a,b and log per-cell slopes; if the
  range exceeds 0.2, report a per-bucket proposal rather than switching
  silently.
