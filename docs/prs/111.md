## Blocking evidence-floor projection (do not start the run)

At t=1.5 the smoke run's best cell has 36 fired high and 15 fired low
decisions; totals are 479 high and 146 low over 24 cells. The planned
4,000-shallow + 1,000-deep run is 3.33× the shallow smoke count, so the best
shallow cell projects to about 120 high / 50 low fired decisions. The
200-per-direction floor therefore cannot be met by construction.

Options for the design decision:

1. Lower the per-cell evidence floor to 50 per direction and keep the
   4,000 + 1,000 plan. A 0/50 CP upper bound is 5.8%, still compatible with
   the 12% false-cut ceiling.
2. Raise shallow calibration to about 16,000 positions (plus deep positions),
   which projects to 200 low decisions in the best cell; this is a materially
   longer run.
3. Change the cell granularity (fewer depth/stage cells) and re-derive the
   floor, which needs a new pre-registration before any run.
