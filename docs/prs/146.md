## Tool: `tools/examples/probcut_fit.rs collect`

- Unit `n` (deterministic in `n` and `--seed`): a WTHOR game and a root
  position with 26–58 empties, both drawn uniformly; a plain alpha-beta
  search of depth 8 from the root with the engine's `ordered_moves`
  (evaluation order from depth 4, as the engine); one visited node with
  remaining depth ≥ 3 and at least 16 empties kept by reservoir sampling.
- For that node: the static value and `search_tt` full-width values at
  depths 1..=D (`endgame_threshold` 0, table cleared once per node),
  D = `--depth`, or `--deep-depth` for every `--deep-every`th unit.
- Split by game: `mix(game ^ seed) % 10 < 7` is `fit`, else `held`.
- One journal line per unit (`othello_tools::journal`), one thread per
  job with its own 64 MB table; resumes by re-running the same command;
  `--nodes` and `--jobs` are not in the header, so a larger `--nodes`
  extends a finished run. The header binds depths, seed, sampling
  constants, the WTHOR file set (hash of sizes and contents), commit and
  binary hash.

## `probcut_fit fit` (written during the run, in `../othello-146-dev`)

Reads a `collect` journal. For each shallow rule (`v1` =
`2·⌊d/4⌋ + (d mod 2)`, `d−4`, `d−6`), depth 3–16 and empties bucket
(16–23, 24–31, 32–39, 40–47, 48–60), it collects `(v_s, v_d)` pairs per
split and prints per cell: counts, the spread of `v_d − v_s` on both
splits, the fitted slope and intercept, and the held-out residual spread.
For rule `v1` it fits the design's three σ forms on the fit split
(linear with `a = 1, b = 0`; linear with pooled `a, b`; per-bucket linear
in `d`), inflates each by the smallest factor that puts model σ at or above
95% of the held-out σ in every cell with at least `--min-cell` pairs in
both splits, and reports the held-out boundary error rates at `--t`
(the share of pairs with `v_d` beyond `a·v_s + b ∓ t·σ`), the worst
cell's 95% Wilson upper bound, mean σ and the inflated constants.

Tests (`cargo test --release --example probcut_fit`, 4 passed): regression
and spread recover a known line and σ on synthetic data; least squares
solves an exact system; Wilson bounds; every shallow rule gives
`1 ≤ s < d`, with even `d − s` for `v1` and beyond depth 6.

Dry run on the first 138 calibration units (copied from the running
journal, `--min-cell 10`): σ around 230–650 centidiscs per cell; the
pipeline runs end to end. No conclusions from this sample.

The probe-type replay (option B) waits for the design verdict.

## `fit` revised for design revision 2

Branch rebased onto #145's revision 2 (`0d36d61`); the calibration commit
`d2ec4a4` that the running job was built from is tagged
`probcut2-calibration` (its tool source is identical to the rebased
`68b48ec`: `git diff d2ec4a4 68b48ec -- tools` is empty).

`fit` now follows the design's § Calibration data, § Supported domain and
§ σ model:

- Rejects a record whose value count is not `depth + 1`.
- Keys each record by its canonical (D4) board, drops repeats (lowest unit
  id kept), and splits by a hash of the key: 60% fit, 20% selection, 20%
  audit; the journal's `split` field is ignored.
- Shallow rules `v1` (from depth 3), `d−4` (from 5), `d−6` (from 7).
- Forms: `a = 1, b = 0` and pooled positive `a, b`, each with
  `σ = c0 + c1·e + c2·d` by weighted least squares on the fit split's
  cells (at least 60 positions).
- On the selection split, for every rectangle of consecutive depths and
  empties buckets (buckets reaching at least threshold + 2 = 20 empties):
  inflate to the 95% σ floor over the rectangle, accept it only if every
  cell has at least 60 positions, meets the floor and has proxy boundary
  errors at `t = 1.5` with Wilson upper bounds of at most 15% each way;
  the most cells win, then the smallest mean σ, across rules and forms.
- Prints every cell's counts and spreads per split, each rule and form's
  rectangle, and the chosen `ProbCut { … }` fields (`t` left for tuning;
  `e_min` at least 20, `e_max` at most 56).
- `--audit`, used once after the choice is frozen: per-cell floor and
  proxy rates with Wilson bounds on the audit split; failing cells shrink
  the rectangle to its largest sub-rectangle without them, with no refit.

Tests (`cargo test --release --example probcut_fit`, 7 passed): the four
earlier ones, the shallow rules valid from their least depth, canonical
keys invariant under the symmetries (and transposition checked on a
single square), deduplication keeping the lowest unit with one split, and
rectangle selection excluding a noisy cell on a constructed grid (18 of
24 cells, inflation below 1.1). `tools/tests/probcut_fit.sh` still passes.

Dry run on the first 726 units (copied from the running journal): 724
distinct; no cell yet has 60 selection positions, so no rectangle, as
expected at this size.

## Review response: tool-stage and fitter re-reviews

All changes in `tools/examples/probcut_fit.rs` and
`tools/tests/probcut_fit.sh`. The production calibration keeps running
from its pinned binary (`d2ec4a4`, tag `probcut2-calibration`); this tool
has a new binary hash and would not resume that journal, by design.

### Tool-stage review

1. **Progress is time-driven.** A reporter thread prints
   `progress done/total elapsed Ns eta Ns` every `--progress` seconds
   (default 30, range 1–86,400; not in the header), and once at the end;
   the count-based lines are gone. Shell regression: a 60-unit run with
   `--progress 1` must print a progress line with fewer than 60 units done
   before it completes.
2. **Strict options.** `options` parses the complete argument list and
   rejects unknown, repeated and value-missing options; `ranged` parses
   with checked conversion and an explicit range (`--nodes` 0–1,000,000,
   `--depth` 1–30, `--deep-depth` depth–30, `--deep-every` ≥ 1, `--jobs`
   1–64, `--seed` any `u64`, `--progress` 1–86,400). All of it happens
   before `Journal::open`. `fit` takes exactly `--in FILE`, `--dev FILE`
   (both required) and the flag `--audit`. Shell regressions, each exiting
   non-zero with no journal created: `--seeeed 8`, a trailing `--depth`,
   `--depth 4294967302`, a repeated `--nodes`, `--jobs 0`; and `fit` with
   a valueless `--dev`. The extension and job-count cases still pass.
3. **Emitted coefficients equal the checked model.** See fitter finding 2:
   the model is quantized to the runtime integers before selection and
   audit, and those integers are what is printed.
   - Non-blocking: `cpu_s` is now `unit_wall_s` (sum of per-unit
     measurement wall times, sampling excluded). Test names have at most
     two underscores. The repeated iterative search in `measure` is
     unchanged, because the running job's labels come from it.

### Fitter re-review

1. **Audit evidence.** An audit cell with fewer than 60 positions
   (including none) is `UNSUPPORTED` and counts as failed, like a floor
   failure; failed cells shrink the rectangle by the rectangle order, and
   if nothing survives `fit --audit` exits non-zero. Before the audit the
   selection is printed as `candidate (selection fold, not audited)`; the
   `ProbCut { … }` line is printed only after a successful audit, as
   `audited ProbCut { … }`, for the surviving rectangle with recomputed
   empties bounds. Tests `audit_needs_evidence` (empty audit and a
   30-position audit fail; a full one passes unchanged) and
   `audit_failure_shrinks` (one noisy audit cell: 24 cells shrink to 20
   without it).
2. **Exact runtime model.** `Runtime` evaluates what the engine will:
   `σ = clamp(ceil((s0 + s1·e + s2·d) / 100), 1, 12800)` at each
   position's actual empties, `m = ceil(t·σ / 100)`, and the integer
   boundary inequalities with floor and ceiling of `a_milli·v_s / 1000`.
   `quantize` rounds the inflated coefficients, `a_milli` and `b`, and
   rejects (does not clamp) a slope outside 500–1500 thousandths, `|b|`
   over 6,400, or a coefficient outside `i32`. Inflation steps until every
   cell meets the floor with the quantized model. The floor compares the
   least runtime σ over the empties range a cell's positions span (σ is
   monotone in `e`) with 0.95 times the residual spread under the
   quantized line. Underidentified forms (fewer cells than coefficients,
   or a singular system) are rejected by `least_squares`. Tests:
   `runtime_arithmetic` (σ rounding and clamps, `m`, both boundaries,
   signed floor and ceiling), `slope_two_unrepresentable` (an exact
   `v_d ≈ 2·v_s` relation fits slope about 2 and is rejected; 1.5 is
   accepted; `b = 6400.6` is rejected), `bucket_edge_floor` (passes at
   the midpoint, fails at the edge `e = 31`), `emitted_round_trip`
   (parsing the printed line gives the same model and the same acceptance
   per cell), `least_squares_identifies` (single-bucket and too-few-rows
   systems rejected).
3. **Audit labels cannot change the choice.** `choose` reads only the fit
   and selection folds; test `audit_labels_inert` perturbs every audit
   sample's values and gets the same rule, form, rectangle and model.
