## Fresh opening set

`tools/examples/gen_openings.rs` now builds both fresh sets with one
function, `fresh_set` (round-robin across 4-ply canonical groups, skipping
canonical end positions already used, as before), and writes them with
`write_fresh`: first `openings_probcut2.txt` (disjoint from the three
frozen sets), then `bench/openings_probcut3.txt` (1,024 openings,
disjoint from the frozen sets and probcut2). Regenerating rewrote the
tune, validation, final and probcut2 files byte-identically (`git status`
shows only the new file and the tool).

Tests (`tools/tests/openings.rs`, 5 passed): `gate_set_is_disjoint`
(1,024 distinct canonical end positions, disjoint from all four existing
sets); `gate_set_is_spread` (the first 400 openings, which the gate plays,
span at least 20 groups with no group above 10%). The existing tests
(frozen hashes, probcut2 disjointness and spread) still pass.

## Pre-registration (binding)

- Candidate: `ProbCut::FITTED` with the `t` picked by #148's registered
  tuning rule, committed into `FITTED` before the gate starts.
- Command, from a pinned worktree at the gate commit, after the machine
  load is below 2:

  ```sh
  othello-tools match --a pc:<t>:3000 --b pc:off:3000 \
    --openings bench/openings_probcut3.txt --pairs 400 --seed 149 \
    --jobs 4 --max-load 6 --out docs/tuning/probcut2_gate.journal
  ```

  The log starts with commit, binary SHA-256, date and load; the same
  command resumes after an interruption; void pairs above load 6 are
  replayed by the tool.
- One look, 400 pairs, no extension.
- Rule: enable only if the lower end of the 95% pair-bootstrap interval of
  A's score is above 0.500 under the repository's degeneracy policy (an
  uninformative interval cannot enable). Enabling is a separate commit
  (`standard()` gets `probcut: Some(ProbCut::FITTED)`) after the results
  review. Otherwise the switch stays off, the result is recorded, and no
  other `t` or model is tried on this set.
- Reported, not gated: thinking time per side from the journal; depth
  completed at 3 s with and without ProbCut on the gate's first 50
  openings after 20 plies, and ProbCut tries and cuts per move, from a
  separate short measurement after the match.

Expected duration: about 6.5 h (the #142 match took 1.6 h for 100 pairs
at 3 s with 4 jobs).
