## Pre-registration (binding)

- Candidate: `ProbCut::FITTED` with the `t` picked by #148's registered
  tuning rule, committed into `FITTED` before the gate starts.
- Command, from a pinned worktree at the gate commit, after the machine
  load is below 2:

  ```sh
  othello-tools match --a pc:<t>:3000 --b pc:off:3000 \
    --openings bench/openings_probcut3.txt --pairs 400 --seed 149 \
    --jobs 4 --max-load 6 --out docs/tuning/probcut2_gate.journal
  ```

  The log starts with commit, binary SHA-256, date and load; the same
  command resumes after an interruption; void pairs above load 6 are
  replayed by the tool.
- One look, 400 pairs, no extension.
- Rule: enable only if the lower end of the 95% pair-bootstrap interval of
  A's score is above 0.500 under the repository's degeneracy policy (an
  uninformative interval cannot enable). Enabling is a separate commit
  (`standard()` gets `probcut: Some(ProbCut::FITTED)`) after the results
  review. Otherwise the switch stays off, the result is recorded, and no
  other `t` or model is tried on this set.
- Reported, not gated: thinking time per side from the journal; depth
  completed at 3 s with and without ProbCut on the gate's first 50
  openings after 20 plies, and ProbCut tries and cuts per move, from a
  separate short measurement after the match.

Expected duration: about 6.5 h (the #142 match took 1.6 h for 100 pairs
at 3 s with 4 jobs).

## Review response: setup corrections (diagnostic data source, load validation)

### Diagnostic protocol amended (non-gated)

The `match` journal records neither moves after the opening nor thinking
time, so the earlier diagnostic could not be collected from it. It is
replaced by a separate, fully specified measurement, fixed now, before any
gate game, and still not part of the gate:

- **Positions:** each of the first 50 `openings_probcut3.txt` openings
  (8 plies), continued to 20 plies by the full-width standard engine
  (`probcut: None`) at fixed depth 6 for both sides, table cleared before
  each move, lowest square on ties as `search_tt` already does: a
  deterministic sample, independent of the gate games.
- **Depth:** for each position, `search_tt` for 3,000 ms with a fresh
  table, once with `pc:<t>`'s configuration and once with `pc:off`'s;
  report each side's completed depths and medians.
- **ProbCut work:** for each position, a fixed-depth `negamax` with a
  fresh table and the `pc:<t>` configuration at the depth that side
  completed in 3 s, reporting `Ctx::probcut_tries` and both cut counters.
- **Thinking time** per side is dropped: the journal does not record it,
  and the gate result does not depend on it.

It will be implemented as a `probcut_screen` subcommand (tools example,
exempt from the tools budget) after #148 merges, and reviewed before it is
run. No change to the candidate, the fresh set, 400 pairs, one look, seed
149, controls, the interval and degeneracy rule, or the separate
enablement.

### Load validation

The `match` driver now applies the load rule to fresh pairs as well as
resumed ones, replaying void pairs and failing without a summary after ten
void passes (#148, `tools/src/main.rs`, test `fresh_overload_void`). The
gate runs only from a build that contains that fix, after #148 merges.
