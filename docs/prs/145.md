# 145: ProbCut v2 design

Design-only issue (no test stage). Epic #144.

- New `docs/design/probcut2.md`, revision 1: a fresh design that cites v1's
  evidence and lessons rather than reviving tag `probcut-v1`.
- `docs/PLAN.md`: records the user's decision of 14 Nov exempting the
  ProbCut fitting tools from the tools budget, quoted.

Decisions put to the reviewer:

1. **Probe type:** A (full-width probes and a selective TT domain, as v1)
   or B (recursive selective probes, one TT domain, Multi-ProbCut as Buro
   and Edax). Recommended: B, with the held-out check replaying the runtime
   predictor.
2. **Calibration:** interior nodes of depth-10 searches from WTHOR roots,
   iterative deepening to 14 (12,000 nodes) and 16 (1,500), about 3 h on 6
   cores; `D_MAX` 16 with no extrapolation.
3. **Tuning:** `t ∈ {0.8, 1.2, 1.6, 2.0}` at 500 ms, 200 pairs each, after
   a node-saving and depth screen.
4. **Gate:** 3 s per move, 400 pairs, fresh `openings_probcut3.txt`; enable
   if the lower 95% bound is above 0.500, one pre-registered extension to
   800 pairs.
5. **Engine budget:** both options exceed the 1,150 working guard (about
   1,175 or 1,195 lines); proposed to raise it to 1,200 for this epic, a
   decision for the user.

Measurements cited in the design (14 Nov, scratch, current engine): depth
completed at 3 s on 11 corpus positions with 22–44 empties: 12–17;
cumulative iterative-deepening time to depth 14: 0.4–7.6 s, to depth 16:
1.8–105 s, on four corpus positions (44, 36, 28, 24 empties).

Ready for design review.

## Review response: design review of `bf8fa02` with `70eb618`

`docs/design/probcut2.md` is now revision 2; its § Review response maps
each finding to the change. In short:

1. Decision replay: the collection record (board, unit and game id,
   completed full-width values) is stated; the proxy check is labelled a
   proxy with defined boundary windows; the runtime replay calls the
   engine's own public `probcut` check on injected candidate models at
   `β = v_d + 1` / `α = v_d − 1`, with fresh cold tables, after the engine
   exists.
2. Supported domain: one evidence unit (depth, empties bucket), no pooling
   across depths, Wilson bounds per cell, a compiled four-bound rectangle
   with no holes, three splits (60/20/20) by canonical position with
   deduplication; the audit split is used once.
3. Table lifecycle: one table per search policy, cleared on any change,
   each caller named (the GUI gains a clear on settings change);
   off-switch identity holds under that precondition; baselines explicitly
   off.
4. Order: screen all four candidates, tune survivors, fixed tie and
   none-survive rules; the 1.5 proxy check is a model filter only.
5. Gate: one look, 400 pairs, no extension.
6. `1 ≤ s < d` per rule; positive `a` in thousandths, clamps on every
   field, conservative signed division; #143's correction carried in;
   epic #144's budget wording updated; `tests/budget.rs` formatting fixed
   (`cargo fmt --check` passes).
