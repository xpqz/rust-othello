# Issue 71: Add differential, perft, symmetry, and invariant tests

## RED

Issue: #71 — Add differential, perft, symmetry, and invariant tests.

Tests added or completed:

- `tests/movegen.rs`: `differential_10k` runs 10,000 seeded positions from
  random playouts; at every position it compares `Naive::legal_moves` with
  production `legal_moves`, and at every legal move it compares `flips` and
  the post-move boards. `differential_200k` is the release-only version.
- `tests/invariants.rs`: `random_invariants` runs 100 seeded playouts through
  the shared `common::playout` and checks `me & opp == 0`, every legal move
  flips at least one disc and adds exactly one disc, and a pass from a
  non-terminal no-move position always leaves the other side with a move (so
  two consecutive passes reach a terminal).
- `tests/perft.rs`: already covers depths 1-8, release-only 9-10, `perft(0)`,
  the pass ply, and the early-terminal rule from #68.
- `tests/symmetry.rs`: already covers D4 orders, compositions, inverses, and
  move-generation commutation from #69.
- The start opening moves `{c4, d3, e6, f5}` are asserted by `start_moves`
  (production) and `start_opening_moves` (reference).

Expected failure: none. The production board passed #66/#67 and the reference
was verified in #70; this issue closes the Phase 1 gate with coverage rather
than changing production code.

## Intended behaviour

- Random playouts drive both the differential and invariant checks through the
  shared `common::playout`.
- The differential compares every legal move, not just the chosen one.
- Invariants hold at every visited position and across every legal transition.
- The heavy differential run uses the same release-only
  `cfg_attr(debug_assertions, ignore = "release-only")` pattern as
  `perft_start_deep`, so `cargo test --release` runs both.

## Review responses

- `random_invariants` now uses `common::playout`; its private pick/play/pass
  loop is gone, and the tautological `is_terminal` restatement is removed.
- The differential now compares `flips` directly as well as the post-move
  boards.
- Mutation check, reverted: changing `Dir::E` to `Dir(1, !0)` (dropping
  `NOT_A`) makes `differential_10k` fail at position 29, square 22, on the
  `flips` comparison. `legal_fixtures`, `shift_edges`, `flips_fixtures`, and
  `shift_masks` also fail; `start_moves` and `start_flips` still pass.
- `differential_200k` now uses the release-only `cfg_attr` pattern.
