# Issue 71: Add differential, perft, symmetry, and invariant tests

## RED

Issue: #71 — Add differential, perft, symmetry, and invariant tests.

Tests added or completed:

- `tests/movegen.rs`: `differential_10k` runs 10,000 seeded positions from
  random playouts; at every position it compares `Naive::legal_moves` with
  production `legal_moves` and compares the production and reference boards
  after every legal move. `differential_200k` is the `#[ignore]` release-only
  version.
- `tests/invariants.rs`: `random_invariants` plays 100 seeded games and checks
  `me & opp == 0`, terminal iff neither side has a move, every legal move
  flips at least one disc and adds exactly one disc, a pass always leaves the
  other side with a move (so two consecutive passes reach a terminal), and
  terminal positions end the game.
- `tests/perft.rs`: already covers depths 1-8, release-only 9-10, `perft(0)`,
  the pass ply, and the early-terminal rule from #68.
- `tests/symmetry.rs`: already covers D4 orders, compositions, inverses, and
  move-generation commutation from #69.
- The start opening moves `{c4, d3, e6, f5}` are asserted by `start_moves`
  (production) and `start_opening_moves` (reference).

Expected failure: none. The production board passed #66/#67 and the reference
was verified in #70; this issue closes the Phase 1 gate with coverage rather
than changing production code.

## Intended behaviour

- Random playouts drive both the differential and invariant checks through the
  shared `common::playout`.
- The differential compares every legal move, not just the chosen one.
- Invariants hold at every visited position and across every legal transition.
- The heavy differential run is `#[ignore]` and release-only in practice.

## Measurements

- Release `differential_200k` runs in about 0.59 s.
- Release perft(8) is about 0.01 s, roughly 39 M nodes/s on this machine;
  PLAN.md's 0.2 s budget is met.
