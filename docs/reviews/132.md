## Findings

No blocking issue in the epic itself. Targeting the sampled hot paths,
keeping results identical and measuring each change is a sensible use of
the remaining line budget. Deferring parallelism keeps this experiment
interpretable. The elapsed-time gap at nominal depth 10 supports measuring
speed opportunities; it does not establish that per-node cost explains the
whole gap, because the programs count different work. The linked task now
states that limitation correctly.

Non-blocking suggestion: before closing this epic, summarize the starting
and final elapsed times on the same fixed workload, production LOC, and
the remaining sampled bottlenecks. Treat compiler vectorization and
microbenchmark gains as supporting evidence; the decision should follow
repeatable whole-search timings. The benchmark defect identified in
`docs/reviews/133.md` must be addressed in that task before its 5% gate is
used.
