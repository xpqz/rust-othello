### Wrong or missing

1. **Stage files are not synced.** `save_stage` writes the temporary file

   no contents. (Moot if the restart protocol is dropped; see the end.)

   needs it. Remove it; an empty held-out set should be an error at the

4. **`family_perm` returns the first symmetry that preserves the square

   code is right today only because the 180° turn comes earlier in the
   list. Skip symmetries whose permutation is the identity.
5. **The production constants were picked to pass the toy tests.** Step
   0.001 with floor 500 means any entry seen fewer than 500 times in a
   stage barely moves. For the two 59,049-entry families that is most
   entries. These must be chosen on held-out error from real labels, as
   the design says, and the choice shown in the notes.

## Checkpoint 2 review, round 1: labelling approved; sweep ruling

Verdict: **labelling approved; constants ruled; proceed to the real fit.**

### Labelling

Accepted. 137,135 labels and 413 rejected match `wthor_check.log`. The
3,600 s interruption and the 12 s resume are useful evidence that the
journal restarts cleanly; keep both parts in the log as committed.

### Sweep

Stopping instead of bending either rule was right. The conflict is real and
it is my rule that was wrong, not the sweep.

Why they conflict: the floor damps a rare entry only over a finite horizon.
The damping bound depends on `step × max_passes / floor` alone (it must be
at most about 0.119), while the passes a stage needs to converge also scale
with `floor / step`. Stage 11 needs slightly more than the bound allows at
every setting, so "passes damping" and "no stage at the maximum" cannot
both hold. The maximum is therefore part of the regulariser, not only a
safety stop.

What the curves show (I read the per-pass curve in the sweep output for
0.02/200): stage 11 held-out MAE is 3.67 at pass 499 and 3.66 from pass 799
to 999. Across all twelve candidates stage 11 ends between 3.6590 and
3.6649. Hitting the maximum there costs nothing measurable.

Ruling:

1. Constants: **step 0.02, floor 200, patience 100, max 1,000.** Damping
   bound 2.59. Mean held MAE 9.9843, within 0.001 of the best candidate,
   and the fit takes about six minutes, so it stays under the ten-minute
   rule without a restart protocol. The two slower floor-200 candidates gain
   0.0005 discs and would cross that rule.
2. Replace "no stage stops at the maximum" with: a stage may stop at the
   maximum if its held-out MAE improved by less than 0.01 discs over the
   last 100 passes. The report prints that figure for each such stage, and
   the fit fails if it is larger. Stages 1 and 11 are expected to qualify.
3. Record in the design document (Fitting) one short paragraph: the
   maximum is the damping horizon, with the `step × max / floor` relation.
4. Set these as `Options::default()`; `damping` must pass unchanged.

One thing to explain in the notes, not fix: stage 0 stops with its best at
pass 0 in every candidate, and held MAE rises from the first pass. I
expect this is the squared-error fit pulling toward the mean label while
the report measures absolute error, on positions with almost no signal.
Confirm or correct that in one sentence.

Then continue with the rest of checkpoint 2 as set before: the real fit,
`assets/eval.bin`, `assets/eval.layout`, `docs/tuning/pattern_fit.log`,
the asset SHA-256 in the notes, and `inspect` for the edge family at early,
