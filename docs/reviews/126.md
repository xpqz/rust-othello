Fixes:

1. **`bench/corpus.txt` provenance.** The third column was regenerated
   but the header still says "Regenerated for the Phase 7 tuned evaluator
   (#99, disc term removed)", and the file's own rule is "Regenerate only
   with a provenance note when the evaluator changes". Replace that line
   with a note naming #126, `pattern::evaluate` and the asset SHA-256
   prefix.
2. **`tt_warm_nodes` was weakened to pass.** The assertion went from
   `warm.nodes * 2 < cold.nodes` to `warm.nodes < cold.nodes`. I measured
   it: 3,908 warm against 7,646 cold from the start position at depth 8,
   a ratio of 0.51, which just misses the old bound. The cause is benign:
   the first two stages' tables are close to zero, so the opening search
   is full of ties and the table saves less. But "fewer nodes" would pass
   with a table that barely works. Move the test to a midgame position
   where the tables carry signal (a corpus board with 30 or more discs
   and at least 15 empties) and restore the factor of two. If that
   position does not reach a factor of two either, stop and report the
   numbers instead of lowering the bound.

Noted, no change required: `fail_soft` now pins 0 at depth 1 from the
start, for the same reason (stage 0 tables are near zero). It still tests
the fail-soft property.
